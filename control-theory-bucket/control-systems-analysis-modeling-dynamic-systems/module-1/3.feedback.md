# Why Use Feedback?

## Open-Loop Control

### No assumed modeling error:

$ y = 10(u - 0.5w) \rightarrow y= 10(\frac{r}{10} - 0.5w)$

$ y = r - 5w$

### With modeling error:

$ y = 9(u - 0.5w) \rightarrow y= 9(\frac{r}{10} - 0.5w)$

$ y = \frac{9}{10}r - 4.5w$

## Closed-Loop Control or Feedback Control

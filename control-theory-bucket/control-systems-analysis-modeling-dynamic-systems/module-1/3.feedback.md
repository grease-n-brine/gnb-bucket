# Why Use Feedback?

## Open-Loop Control

Experimental

### No assumed modeling error:

$y = 10(u - 0.5w) \rightarrow y= 10(\frac{r}{10} - 0.5w)$

$y = r - 5w$

### With modeling error:

$y = 9(u - 0.5w) \rightarrow y= 9(\frac{r}{10} - 0.5w)$

$y = \frac{9}{10}r - 4.5w$

## Closed-Loop Control or Feedback Control

###  No assumed modeling error:

$y_c = 10[u - 0.5w] \rightarrow y_c = 1[10(r-y_c)]-0.5w$

$y_c = \frac{100}{101} r - \frac{5}{101}w$
